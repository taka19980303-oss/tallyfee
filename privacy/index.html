Privacy policy

Short version: there is no account, no form, and no server-side record of your numbers. Every calculation runs in your browser.

What stays in your browser

Calculator inputs are never sent to a server. To make results shareable and resumable, calculator pages may store your last inputs in your own browser's local storage and mirror them into the page address (URL parameters). A link you copy therefore pre-fills the same numbers for whoever opens it — that is the only place your inputs ever go. To remove them, clear your browser storage or edit the address.

Analytics

This site is preparing to use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a cookie-less analytics service: it counts pageviews without setting cookies and without fingerprinting visitors. The event scaffolding on these pages records outbound-link clicks and calculator completions in your browser's memory only — an in-page queue that is never transmitted anywhere. If the analytics beacon is enabled, pageview information is processed by Cloudflare under its own privacy policy.

Outbound links

Links to vendors and platforms take you to their sites, which may set their own cookies — including, once affiliate links are active, attribution cookies used to credit a referral. Those cookies are set by the destination site, not by this one, and are governed by that site's privacy policy.
