How this site makes money

This site is free to use. Part of its running costs are covered by affiliate commissions — and the rules below are published before any affiliate link is switched on, so the terms are fixed in advance.

Current status

As of November 23, 2026, the vendor links on this site are plain official links and no affiliate links are active yet. When affiliate links are introduced, two things will appear in the same update: a disclosure banner at the top of every page that contains one, and an (Ad) label next to each affiliate CTA. The wording is the text in the next two sections.

The page-top banner

“Advertising disclosure: This page contains affiliate links. If you sign up or buy through them, we may earn a commission from the vendor — at no extra cost to you. Commissions never change the prices we list, our rankings or our verdicts: every figure is read on the vendor's official page and stamped with the date it was checked.”

Next to each CTA

“Official pricing & signup ↗ (affiliate link)” — the label travels with the CTA itself; a link to this page alone is not the disclosure.

The detail

This site is free to use. Part of its running costs are covered by affiliate commissions: when you sign up or buy through some of the links on our software pages, the vendor may pay us a referral fee via PartnerStack, Impact or Trackdesk. The fee is paid by the vendor and never added to your price. Our calculations, rankings and 'cheapest' verdicts are based on price data only. Whether a vendor pays us — and how much — plays no part in them, and products with no affiliate program are listed and compared on exactly the same basis. Every price is read on the vendor's official page and dated; see each page for its source and check date. If you spot an error, contact us at [contact email to be added].
