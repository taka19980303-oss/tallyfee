How to read this

Fees are only half the decision — audience, sell-through speed and shipping programmes differ by platform, and none of that is modelled here. What is modelled, exactly, is the fee schedule each platform publishes: eBay’s tiered final value fee, Etsy’s four-part stack, Poshmark’s flat 20%, Mercari’s flat 10%, Amazon’s category referral fee (Everything Else, 15%, in this comparison), Depop’s and Vinted’s zero seller fee, and Whatnot’s commission-plus-processing. Facebook Marketplace and TikTok Shop are not in this comparison — Facebook’s fee turns on checkout vs local pickup and TikTok’s runs by category group, so each has its own calculator instead: the Facebook Marketplace fee calculator · the TikTok Shop fee calculator.
