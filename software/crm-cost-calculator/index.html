Starting free instead?

This calculator prices paid plans — but two of the five CRMs have a free tier worth starting on: HubSpot Sales Hub — Up to 2 users, unlimited contacts · Zoho CRM — Forever free for up to 3 users. Pipedrive, Freshsales, Close are trial-only. At one to three seats, the free tiers may be all you need for now.

What the seat price leaves out

Onboarding. HubSpot charges a mandatory one-time onboarding fee on Professional ($1,500) and Enterprise ($3,500). It is included in the year-one and 3-year totals above — most comparison sites leave it out.

Seat types. HubSpot sells cheaper Core Seats alongside Sales Seats, so a mixed team pays less than the all-Sales-Seat figure here. Close's Solo plan is limited to one user, so the calculator skips it as soon as you enter 2+ seats.

Unpublished prices stay unpublished. Zoho's monthly-billing USD rates and HubSpot Enterprise's monthly rate are not on the vendors' pricing pages — pick monthly billing and those rows show no monthly figure at all, just a note with the annual-billing price for reference.
