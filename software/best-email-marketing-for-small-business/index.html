Best email marketing for a small business, by the numbers

At 5,000 contacts, the verified prices say the quiet part out loud: MailerLite’s cheapest plan is $44.10/month, and Brevo prices the same list at $28.75/month if you send 20,000 emails a month — because it bills on sends, not list size. The right pick depends on which of those two numbers describes your business.

✓ Prices verifiedNovember 23, 2026 — every price on this page was read on the vendor's official pricing page. Promotional prices are never used as the list price.
Most transparent pricing: MailerLite
Full published tier table, active subscribers only, $44.10/month at 5,000 subscribers billed annually (Comfort plan). The catch: counting is cumulative inside a billing cycle.
Big list, rare sends: Brevo
Bills on send volume. 20,000 emails a month costs $28.75/month billed annually on the Starter plan — and storing up to 500,000 contacts is included. Mail a huge list twice a year and Brevo wins easily.
Automation-first: ActiveCampaign
The strongest automation of the six, priced from $49/month billed annually at 1,000 contacts (Plus). New accounts count every contact — budget for your full list, not your active list.
The default, with a warning: Mailchimp
Fine tool, slippery bill: unsubscribed contacts count until archived, and billing uses your peak list size in the period. Archive aggressively or pay for ghosts.
