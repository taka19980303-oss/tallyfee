How Mailchimp counts

Counts every contact in your audience — including unsubscribed and non-subscribed contacts — until you archive them. Your bill is based on the PEAK contact total in the billing period.

Going over your limit

Pass your plan limit and Mailchimp auto-charges for add-on contact blocks (for example $22 per 1,000-contact block on Essentials at the 10,000 tier). Sending is never interrupted.
