How MailerLite counts

Counts ACTIVE subscribers only — but cumulatively within a billing cycle: an address that was active at any point in the cycle counts, even if you delete it later.

Going over your limit

No per-contact fee. Cross your tier and MailerLite auto-upgrades you to the next tier and charges the card (with an in-account warning first).

Plans were renamed in June 2026: Comfort was “Growing Business”, Power was “Advanced”.

How does MailerLite count contacts?

Counts ACTIVE subscribers only — but cumulatively within a billing cycle: an address that was active at any point in the cycle counts, even if you delete it later.
