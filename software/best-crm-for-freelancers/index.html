Best CRM for freelancers, by the numbers

One seat changes everything: two of these five are free or nearly free at a single seat, and the most expensive-looking one is actually annual-only. Verified prices, one seat, billed annually.

✓ Prices verifiedNovember 23, 2026 — every price on this page was read on the vendor's official pricing page. Promotional prices are never used as the list price.
Free and enough: HubSpot Free CRM
Up to 2 users, unlimited contacts, $0. For a freelancer, the free tier is the product — upgrade only when pipelines multiply.
Cheapest paid seat: Zoho or Close Solo
Zoho Standard is $14/month billed annually; Close Solo is $9/month billed annually but caps you at 10,000 leads and one user — which, solo, you are.
Skip: anything with onboarding fees
HubSpot Professional’s $1,500 mandatory onboarding is 14 months of Zoho. At one seat, enterprise machinery is just overhead.
