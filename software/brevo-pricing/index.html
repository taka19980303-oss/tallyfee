How Brevo counts

Brevo is the outlier: it bills on monthly SEND VOLUME, not list size. Every tier shown includes storage for up to 500,000 contacts.

Going over your limit

No per-email overage rate is published. Extra volume is bought as prepaid email credit packs (5,000–1,000,000 emails).

Plan names changed: the mid plan is now called Standard (there is no “Business” plan anymore).
