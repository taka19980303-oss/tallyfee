Best email marketing for creators, by the numbers

Creators live on two numbers: how big the free tier is, and whether sends are capped. On verified prices, one tool gives you 10,000 subscribers and unlimited sends for $0 — and it is not the famous one.

✓ Prices verifiedNovember 23, 2026 — every price on this page was read on the vendor's official pricing page. Promotional prices are never used as the list price.
Best free tier: Kit
The free Newsletter plan covers up to 10,000 subscribers with unlimited sends. Paid Creator starts at $39/month at 1,000 subscribers. One trap: downgrades are manual — you email support after cleaning your list.
Cheapest paid start: MailerLite
Comfort starts at $12/month for 500 subscribers, and only active subscribers count. If your list is small but engaged, nothing else is close.
If you sell by email volume: Brevo
300 free emails a day forever, and paid plans priced on sends. Good for launch-style sending to a list you built elsewhere.
Skip for now: Constant Contact
No free plan at all — trial only. For a creator testing the waters, that is a needless paywall.
