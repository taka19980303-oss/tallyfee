Best CRM for a small business, by the numbers

For a 5-seat team billed annually, verified year-one totals run from $540.00 (HubSpot, cheapest fitting plan) to $840.00 (Zoho, cheapest plan) — a gap set by seat pricing alone, before any platform premium. Here is how to choose without paying for seats you don’t use.

✓ Prices verifiedNovember 23, 2026 — every price on this page was read on the vendor's official pricing page. Promotional prices are never used as the list price.
Lowest cost: HubSpot
Starter is $9/seat/month billed annually — $540.00 a year for five, with no onboarding fee at this tier. Free CRM for up to 2 users if you are starting out. Watch the step up: Professional adds a mandatory $1,500 onboarding fee.
Simplest bill: Pipedrive
One price per seat, no minimum shown, no onboarding fee. Growth at $39/seat/month billed annually is the sweet spot for a sales-led team.
The platform bet: HubSpot
Free CRM for 2 users is real, and the ecosystem is the deepest here. But Professional adds a mandatory $1,500 onboarding fee, and seat types complicate the bill as you grow.
Phone-heavy teams: Close
Built around calling — which is also its catch: calls and SMS are usage-billed on top of seats. Model your call volume before the trial ends.
