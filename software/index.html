What software really costs

Marketplace fees are only half of a small business's bill. The other half is software — email tools that charge per contact, CRMs that charge per seat. This section totals those up the same way: real prices, read on each vendor's official pricing page, with the date they were checked.

✓ Prices verifiedNovember 23, 2026 — every price on this page was read on the vendor's official pricing page. Promotional prices are never used as the list price.

Why “verified + dated” matters here too

Software pricing pages are moving targets: MailerLite renamed both of its plans in June 2026, Brevo dropped its “Business” plan name, ActiveCampaign changed who counts as a contact in November 2025, and HubSpot's own pages quoted two different Starter prices on the day we checked. Every figure in this section carries its source and check date, and anything a vendor does not publish — like ActiveCampaign's monthly-billing prices — is labelled as not published instead of being guessed.
