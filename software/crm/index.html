CRM pricing, with the seat math done

Per-seat pricing multiplies quietly: the plan price × your team × 12 months, plus the fees that don't appear on the pricing card — onboarding, extra seat types, usage billing. The table below is year-one totals for the cheapest fitting plan, onboarding included where the vendor mandates it.

✓ Prices verifiedNovember 23, 2026 — every price on this page was read on the vendor's official pricing page. Promotional prices are never used as the list price.

The traps, per tool

HubSpot Sales Hub
Two seat types: the prices here are Sales Seats. Cheaper Core Seats ($50/mo on Professional, $75 on Enterprise) exist for users who do not need the full sales tools — so your real bill depends on the seat MIX, not just headcount. Professional and Enterprise carry a mandatory one-time onboarding fee ($1,500 / $3,500), included in the year-1 totals in the calculator.
Pipedrive
Straight per-seat pricing, VAT excluded. No seat minimum was shown on the pricing page. The upsell is add-ons: LeadBooster, Campaigns and Web Visitors are priced separately and are not on the pricing page.
Zoho CRM
Per user. Watch the bundle trap: Zoho One’s “All Employee Pricing” requires licensing every employee on payroll (a Flexible User option exists).
Freshsales
Per user. No free plan on the current pricing page — 21-day trial only. Metered add-ons sit on top: Freddy AI Agent sessions ($49 per 100 sessions after a one-time 500 free) and Branded Documents ($19/user/mo).
Close
Per user. Solo is restricted to a single user and 10,000 leads. The seat price is not the whole bill: calling and SMS are usage-billed (about $0.02/min outbound), the Call Assistant add-on is $50/mo per account, and extra organizations are $50/mo each.
monday CRM
Priced by billing country. From Japan the official page shows yen pricing with a 3-seat minimum — full table on the monday pricing page.
