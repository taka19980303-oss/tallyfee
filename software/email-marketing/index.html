Email marketing tools, priced honestly

The same 5,000-contact list costs anywhere from $28.75 a month (Brevo) to $79.00 (ActiveCampaign) depending on the tool — and the gap is mostly in how each tool counts a contact and what happens when you cross a tier. Prices below are each tool's cheapest plan at that size.

✓ Prices verifiedNovember 23, 2026 — every price on this page was read on the vendor's official pricing page. Promotional prices are never used as the list price.

New to email marketing?

Start free: Kit is free up to 10,000 subscribers and Brevo sends 300 emails a day free. When you outgrow free, best email marketing for a small business, by the numbers shows which paid plan fits — then price your exact list in the calculator.

How each tool counts a contact

Mailchimp
Counts every contact in your audience — including unsubscribed and non-subscribed contacts — until you archive them. Your bill is based on the PEAK contact total in the billing period.
Brevo
Brevo is the outlier: it bills on monthly SEND VOLUME, not list size. Every tier shown includes storage for up to 500,000 contacts.
Kit
Counts unique ACTIVE subscribers only — unsubscribes stop counting. Sends are unlimited on every plan.
ActiveCampaign
Accounts created on or after Nov 3, 2025 count ALL contacts toward the limit — active, unsubscribed, bounced and unconfirmed alike. Older accounts count active contacts only.
MailerLite
Counts ACTIVE subscribers only — but cumulatively within a billing cycle: an address that was active at any point in the cycle counts, even if you delete it later.
Constant Contact
Tiered by contact count; send allowance is 10×/12×/24× your contacts per month by plan.

Every tool, in full

Mailchimp pricing
The default choice — and the one whose bill surprises people, because unsubscribed contacts still count until you archive them.
Brevo pricing
Bills on emails SENT, not list size. A big list you mail rarely is nearly free here; a small list you mail daily is not.
Kit pricing
Creator-focused. Unlimited sends on every plan and a free tier up to 10,000 subscribers — but downgrades are manual, by email.
ActiveCampaign pricing
The automation heavyweight. New accounts count EVERY contact toward the limit, even unsubscribed and bounced ones.
MailerLite pricing
The transparent one: the full tier table is published, only active subscribers count, and there are no counting tricks in the fine print.
Constant Contact pricing
Simple and long-established, with the gentlest overage ($0.002/send, never blocked) — but no free plan and “Contact Sales” past 50,000.
