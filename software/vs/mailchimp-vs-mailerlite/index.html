Mailchimp vs MailerLite: the real price difference

At 5,000 contacts, Mailchimp’s cheapest plan is $39.21/month (Essentials, billed monthly) and MailerLite’s is $44.10/month (Comfort, billed annual). But list price is only half of it — the two tools count contacts differently, and that changes the bill as your list grows.

Break-even: at every list size in the table, the same tool stays cheaper on list price — the decision then comes down to how each counts contacts and what happens when you cross a tier (see below).

How they count

Mailchimp: Counts every contact in your audience — including unsubscribed and non-subscribed contacts — until you archive them. Your bill is based on the PEAK contact total in the billing period.

MailerLite: Counts ACTIVE subscribers only — but cumulatively within a billing cycle: an address that was active at any point in the cycle counts, even if you delete it later.

Over the limit

Mailchimp: Pass your plan limit and Mailchimp auto-charges for add-on contact blocks (for example $22 per 1,000-contact block on Essentials at the 10,000 tier). Sending is never interrupted.

MailerLite: No per-contact fee. Cross your tier and MailerLite auto-upgrades you to the next tier and charges the card (with an in-account warning first).
