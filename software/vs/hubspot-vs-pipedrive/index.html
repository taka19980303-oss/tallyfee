What the seat price leaves out

HubSpot Sales Hub: Two seat types: the prices here are Sales Seats. Cheaper Core Seats ($50/mo on Professional, $75 on Enterprise) exist for users who do not need the full sales tools — so your real bill depends on the seat MIX, not just headcount. Professional and Enterprise carry a mandatory one-time onboarding fee ($1,500 / $3,500), included in the year-1 totals in the calculator.

Pipedrive: Straight per-seat pricing, VAT excluded. No seat minimum was shown on the pricing page. The upsell is add-ons: LeadBooster, Campaigns and Web Visitors are priced separately and are not on the pricing page.
