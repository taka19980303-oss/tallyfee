What this calculator knows that headline prices don't

Contact counting differs. Mailchimp bills for unsubscribed contacts until you archive them, and bills on your peak list size in the period. ActiveCampaign counts every contact — including bounced and unconfirmed — for accounts created on or after November 3, 2025. MailerLite and Kit count active subscribers only. The same “5,000 contacts” is therefore not the same billable number on every tool; the tier each tool puts you in is what this calculator prices.

Tier jumps are the price. Most of these tools auto-upgrade you the moment you cross a tier — there is no per-contact overage to model, just the next tier's price. Constant Contact and ActiveCampaign instead charge per-send overage fees on top of a send allowance; Mailchimp auto-charges for contact blocks. The plan prices here are the list prices before any overage.

“Not published” is an answer too. ActiveCampaign does not publish monthly-billing prices, and Mailchimp publishes no annual rate card — switch the billing toggle and those rows say so instead of inventing a number.
