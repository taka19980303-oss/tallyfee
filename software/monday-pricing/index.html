monday CRM pricing — the regional-pricing problem

monday prices its CRM by billing country, and its pricing page has no currency switch. Read from Japan on November 23, 2026, the official page showed the yen table below. US-dollar prices could not be confirmed from an official page in that environment — so instead of converting or guessing, this page publishes the yen table as displayed and keeps monday out of the USD calculator until its dollar prices can be verified the same way.

✓ Prices verifiedNovember 23, 2026 — every price on this page was read on the vendor's official pricing page. Promotional prices are never used as the list price.

One number on the page does not add up

The page advertises “Yearly — SAVE 33%”, and its FAQ repeats a 33% annual discount. The displayed prices do not match it: Basic drops from ¥1,860 to ¥1,560 a month, which is about 16% off, not 33%. The table above uses the displayed prices, not the advertised percentage.

What this means if you are comparing CRMs

If your billing country is Japan, the yen table is your real price list. If it is the US, monday's dollar prices exist — they just cannot be read from a Japan-region browser, and this site does not publish prices it could not read on an official page. The other five CRMs in the CRM calculator publish dollar pricing that was verified directly.
