How ActiveCampaign counts

Accounts created on or after Nov 3, 2025 count ALL contacts toward the limit — active, unsubscribed, bounced and unconfirmed alike. Older accounts count active contacts only.

Going over your limit

$0.005 per email sent over the monthly send limit (10×/10×/12×/15× your contact limit by plan). If overage sends reach 3× the limit, sending is switched off until the next month.
