HubSpot Sales Hub pricing, checked against the official page

The platform play: a genuinely free CRM, then per-seat pricing with two seat types and mandatory onboarding fees at the top tiers. Full published per-seat prices below, read on the official pricing page on November 23, 2026.

✓ Prices verifiedNovember 23, 2026 — every price on this page was read on the vendor's official pricing page. Promotional prices are never used as the list price.

What the seat price leaves out

Two seat types: the prices here are Sales Seats. Cheaper Core Seats ($50/mo on Professional, $75 on Enterprise) exist for users who do not need the full sales tools — so your real bill depends on the seat MIX, not just headcount.

Professional and Enterprise carry a mandatory one-time onboarding fee ($1,500 / $3,500), included in the year-1 totals in the calculator.

HubSpot’s own pages disagreed on Starter on the check date: the pricing guide shows $9/seat/mo billed annually, while the official Product & Services Catalog lists a $20/mo list rate per Core Seat. The calculator uses the pricing-page rates.
