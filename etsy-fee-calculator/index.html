How Etsy fees work

Rates verified: November 18, 2026 on Etsy’s official Fees & Payments Policy and Help Center · Listing fee $0.20 · Transaction fee 6.5% of price + shipping (US: sales tax excluded) · Processing 3% + $0.25 on the total including tax · Regulatory operating fee in nine countries only — US sellers are not charged it · Offsite Ads 12%/15%, capped at $100 per order.

The bases differ — that is where most calculators go wrong. The 6.5% transaction fee is charged on the item price plus shipping (and gift wrap), but not on sales tax for US sellers. The 3% processing fee is the one that touches tax: it is charged on the full amount the buyer paid, tax included, plus a flat $0.25. The regulatory operating fee applies in exactly nine countries (Canada, France, Hungary, Italy, India, Spain, Türkiye, the UK and Vietnam); if you sell from the US, that line is $0 — calculators that add it for US sellers overstate your fees.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Etsy charge per sale in 2026?

For a US seller: $0.20 listing fee + 6.5% transaction fee on price + shipping + 3% + $0.25 payment processing on the total the buyer paid (tax included). On a $100 order with free shipping that is $0.20 + $6.50 + $3.25 = $9.95.

Does Etsy’s transaction fee apply to sales tax?

No — for US sellers the 6.5% transaction fee is charged on price + shipping + gift wrap, excluding sales tax. The 3% processing fee does apply to the tax-inclusive total.

What is Etsy’s regulatory operating fee?

An extra percentage Etsy charges sellers in nine countries only (e.g. UK 0.48%, France 1.14%, Hungary 1.97%). The United States is not on the list, so US sellers pay nothing on this line.

How does the Offsite Ads fee work?

If a sale is attributed to an Offsite Ad, Etsy charges 15% (shops under $10k prior-year sales, who can opt out) or 12% (shops at $10k+, mandatory) of price + shipping, capped at $100 per order.
