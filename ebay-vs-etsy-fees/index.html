Why the fees land differently

eBay charges one big percentage — 13.6% of the total amount (price + shipping + tax) up to $7,500 per item — plus a $0.40 per-order fee. Processing is included. On this example: $7.48 + $0.40 = $7.88.

Etsy charges smaller pieces on different bases: $0.20 listing + 6.5% of price + shipping ($3.58) + 3% + $0.25 processing on the total paid ($1.90) = $5.68. No regulatory operating fee applies to US sellers.

When eBay wins instead

High-value items: above $7,500 eBay’s marginal rate drops to 2.35%, while Etsy’s percentages stay flat — the gap narrows and can reverse. Top Rated Plus sellers also take 10% off eBay’s final value fee. And category matters on both sides (eBay media categories are 15.3%; an Etsy sale attributed to Offsite Ads adds 12–15%, capped at $100).
