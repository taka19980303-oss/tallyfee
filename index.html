Why “accurate + dated” is the whole product

1 · Rates change — pages go stale
eBay’s standard rate moved to 13.6% in February 2025 while top-ranked pages still show 13.25% — and many flatten the $7,500 tier away entirely. Etsy revised its regulatory fees in June 2026 and most templates missed it. Every calculator here is confirmed on the platform’s official fee page and carries its verification date.
2 · The correct base for every fee
Fees are not all charged on the same number: eBay charges on price + shipping + tax, Etsy’s transaction fee excludes tax while its processing fee includes it, Whatnot’s commission excludes shipping while its processing includes it. Each calculator applies every fee on its official base, and rounds each component to the cent so the breakdown always adds up.
3 · Fee history is the reference
Each rate change creates fresh search demand and a reason to return. The dated fee-change history — what changed, when, and what it costs per sale — is the record sellers bookmark.
