How Facebook Marketplace fees work

Rates verified: November 23, 2026 on Meta’s official policy page (Marketplace Seller Protection Policy / Commerce Payments Terms — Merchant Policies). Meta’s wording: it charges Individual Sellers “a fee of 10% per transaction, with a minimum fee of $0.80”, and “the Selling Fee is calculated based on the total transaction amount, including the item price, shipping fees, and applicable taxes.” The 10% rate took effect April 15, 2024 (previously 5% with a $0.40 minimum). Shipped checkout orders must use Meta’s prepaid shipping labels to qualify for seller protection.

Why so many pages still say 5%: the doubling to 10% in April 2024 was easy to miss — it changed inside Meta’s payments terms rather than with a headline announcement — and pre-2024 guides and calculators are still the top results in places. If a page quotes 5% for shipped Marketplace sales, it is working from the old schedule.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Facebook Marketplace charge sellers?

For shipped orders sold through Marketplace checkout: 10% of the entire transaction amount — item price plus shipping plus applicable taxes — with a minimum fee of $0.80 per transaction. For local pickup sales paid in person: nothing. There is no listing fee.

Is the fee charged on shipping and tax too?

Yes. Meta’s policy calculates the Selling Fee on the total transaction amount, explicitly including shipping fees and applicable taxes. On a $50 item with $5 shipping and no tax, the fee is 10% of $55 = $5.50 — not 10% of the item alone. Because the tax is included in the base, the fee runs slightly above 10% of the money that is actually yours.

What is the $0.80 minimum?

A floor, not an extra charge: Meta takes whichever is larger, 10% of the transaction or $0.80. On a $5 shipped item, 10% would be $0.50, so the $0.80 minimum applies instead — cheap shipped items lose a much larger effective percentage.

Does the fee apply to local pickup sales?

No. The Selling Fee belongs to transactions processed through Meta’s checkout — shipped orders paid inside Marketplace. A local sale arranged in chat and paid in person (cash or your own payment app) is not such a transaction, so no Selling Fee applies.

Is there a payment processing fee on top?

Not for individual sellers in the policy text: the Selling Fee is the transaction charge Meta defines for Marketplace checkout sales, and no separate processing percentage appears alongside it in the Merchant Policies.
