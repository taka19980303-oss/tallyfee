How Mercari fees work

Rates verified: November 18, 2026 on Mercari’s official US Help Center · Selling fee 10% of the completed item price + buyer-paid shipping · No payment processing fee · Buyer Protection 3.6% is charged to the buyer.

Mercari’s fee structure has flipped twice in two years: in 2024 it removed the seller fee and moved charges to buyers, then on January 6, 2025 it reintroduced a flat 10% seller fee and dropped the separate processing fee. Many pages still describe the 2024 zero-fee structure — check the date on anything you read. The full story is in the Mercari fees guide and the fee-change history.

Frequently asked questions

What is Mercari’s selling fee in 2026?

A flat 10% of the item price plus the shipping the buyer pays, with no separate payment processing fee. Reintroduced on January 6, 2025 and verified on Mercari’s official Help Center on November 18, 2026.

Does Mercari charge sellers a payment processing fee?

No. The January 2025 structure removed it. The only percentage a seller pays is the 10% selling fee.

What is the 3.6% Buyer Protection fee — do I pay it?

The buyer pays it, on top of the price. It is shown in the breakdown above for information only and is never deducted from the seller’s proceeds.

Does the 10% apply to shipping?

Yes — the fee base is the completed item price plus buyer-paid shipping. A $50 item with $5 shipping pays $5.50, not $5.00.
