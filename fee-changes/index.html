Fee-Change History

Every rate change behind the calculators on this site — dated, and linked to the source it was confirmed on. If a change can’t be sourced, it isn’t listed. Current rates were last verified on November 18, 2026.

Sep 21, 2026
Whatnot

Tiered commission structure took effect — US commission now falls in tiers as four-week sales grow; the Standard tier (every new seller) stays at 8% in most verticals and 4% for Coins & Money, with payment processing unchanged at 2.9% + $0.30. The Whatnot calculator uses the Standard tier.

Source: Whatnot Help Center — Whatnot seller fees help.whatnot.com

Jun 22, 2026
Etsy

Regulatory operating fee revised in nine-country schedule — UK 0.32% → 0.48%, France 0.47% → 1.14%, Italy 0.32% → 0.80%, Spain 0.72% → 0.88%, Hungary newly introduced at 1.97%. US sellers remain not charged. Announced April 22, 2026; this is the schedule the Etsy calculator uses today.

Source: Etsy Help Center — What is a Regulatory Operating Fee? help.etsy.com · dated change log: seethefees.com/fee-change-tracker

Feb 14, 2025
eBay

Final value fee, most categories: 13.25% → 13.6% on the first $7,500 per item (2.35% above unchanged). Books/movies/music 14.95% → 15.3%. This is why calculators still showing 13.25% understate your fees today.

Source: eBay January 2025 seller update, as logged with before/after figures at profitvana.com/fee-history/ebay · current schedule: eBay Seller Center — Seller fees ebay.com/sellercenter

Jan 6, 2025
Mercari (US)

Seller fee reintroduced at a flat 10% of the completed item price plus buyer-paid shipping, replacing the 2024 buyer-fee experiment; the separate payment processing fee was removed and a buyer-paid 3.6% Buyer Protection fee introduced. This is the structure the Mercari calculator uses today.

Source: Mercari Help Center — FAQs about our new fee structure mercari.com/help_center · Changes to our fee structure mercari.com/help_center

Oct 24, 2024
Poshmark

Fee structure reverted to $2.95 / 20% — a short-lived structure with a buyer protection fee was rolled back, restoring the flat $2.95 under $15 and 20% at $15 or more that remains in force today.

Source: Revert date and removal of the buyer protection fee: 3dsellers.com/marketplaces/poshmark · current structure: Poshmark Support Center support.poshmark.com

Jul 18, 2024
Depop

US selling fee removed — US sellers stopped paying Depop’s selling fee and buyers began paying a Marketplace fee at checkout instead; the seller-side cost became the Depop Payments processing fee (3.3% + $0.45). The UK had made the same change for new listings in March 2024.

Source: Current schedule and structure: Depop Help Centre — Seller fees and charges depophelp.zendesk.com

Mar 15, 2024
eBay

Per-order fee on orders over $10: $0.30 → $0.40 — its first change since managed payments began. Orders of $10 or less stayed at $0.30.

Source: Dated changelog with the current schedule verified against eBay’s official fee page: net-proceeds.com/ebay-fee-calculator

Apr 11, 2022
Etsy

Transaction fee: 5% → 6.5% of the order total (item price + shipping + gift wrap) — the increase that prompted the April 2022 seller strike. The $0.20 listing fee and US processing rate (3% + $0.25) were unchanged.

Source: Etsy fee history with announcement and SEC-filing sourcing: profitvana.com/fee-history/etsy · current policy: Etsy Fees & Payments Policy etsy.com/legal/fees
