How Poshmark fees work

Rates verified: November 18, 2026 on Poshmark’s official Support Center · Sales under $15: $2.95 flat · Sales of $15 or more: 20% of the item price. Shipping is a buyer-paid prepaid label and is neither your revenue nor part of the fee base.

Poshmark briefly moved to a different structure with a buyer protection fee in October 2024, then reverted within weeks — the $2.95 / 20% structure shown here is the one in force today (see the fee-change history). Note the cliff at $15: a $14.99 sale pays $2.95 (19.7%), while a $15.00 sale pays $3.00 (20%) — below that, the flat fee takes a growing share as prices fall: on a $5 sale, $2.95 is 59% of the price.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Poshmark take from a sale?

$2.95 flat on sales under $15, and 20% of the item price on sales of $15 or more. Rates verified on Poshmark’s official Support Center on November 18, 2026.

Does Poshmark charge commission on shipping?

No. Poshmark shipping is a prepaid label paid by the buyer; it never passes through your balance, so it is not revenue and not part of the commission base.

Is Poshmark’s 20% worth it versus eBay?

It depends on the item — run the same numbers in the 8-platform comparison: on a $50 item with $26 of costs, Poshmark’s flat 20% leaves less than eBay or Etsy, but there is no separate payment processing or per-order fee to model.
