TikTok Shop Fee Calculator

TikTok Shop charges one referral fee per order — in TikTok’s own policy wording, it “encompasses all TikTok Shop fees, with the exception of shipping and tax fees.” There is no separate payment-processing line. The rate runs by category: 6% for most, 5% for fine jewelry and pre-owned, with the portion of a collectible or pre-owned sale over $10,000 at 3% — charged on customer payment + platform discount − tax, a base that includes the shipping the buyer paid.

How TikTok Shop fees work

Rates verified: November 23, 2026 on TikTok Shop Seller University (US) — the category referral table and “Referral Fee Updates” (page updated May 8, 2025). Formula: Referral Fee = rate × (Customer Payment + Platform Discount − Tax), and the base includes the shipping fee paid by the customer. Rates: 6% for most categories; 5% for diamond, gold, jade, platinum / carat gold and ruby / sapphire / emerald jewelry (since October 31, 2024) and for all Pre-Owned categories; Collectibles 6% and Pre-Owned 5%, with any portion of the sale over $10,000 charged at 3% — in this calculator the $10,000 tier is applied to the fee base. TikTok’s own worked example: 6% × ($102 + $5 − $2) = $6.30. Settlement runs after delivery, with a $1 payout threshold.

About the “8%” figures going around: some seller posts and fee blogs describe a rise to 8% in August 2026. TikTok’s official category table, read on November 23, 2026, still lists 6% for standard categories, and TikTok’s policy says material fee changes are notified about 30 days before they take effect. This calculator uses the official table — and your own settlement statement (Seller Center → Finance → Statements) is the final word on what your shop was actually charged.

Frequently asked questions

How much does TikTok Shop take from a sale?

One referral fee: 6% of the fee base for most categories, 5% for fine jewelry and pre-owned items, and 3% on the portion of a collectible or pre-owned sale over $10,000. The fee base is what the customer paid (item + shipping) plus any TikTok-funded platform discount, minus tax. On a $100 item with free shipping and no discount or tax, the fee is $6.00.

Is there a payment processing fee on top?

No. TikTok’s policy defines the referral fee as encompassing all TikTok Shop fees, with the exception of shipping and tax fees — there is no separate processing line on the settlement report. Creator (affiliate) commissions are different: you set that rate yourself, and it is not included here.

What happens to the fee when an order is refunded?

TikTok returns the referral fee minus a Refund Administration Fee of 20% of the referral fee, capped at $5 per SKU since May 15, 2025. TikTok’s own examples: a $10 item at 6% leaves a $0.12 admin fee; a $500 item would be $6.00, reduced to the $5 cap. No admin fee applies to “buy now refund later” creator sample refunds or to eligible cancellations before shipment.

Why do some calculators show 8%?

Seller reports from August 2026 describe an 8% charge, but no official TikTok announcement or updated table has been published that we could find — the official category table read on November 23, 2026 still lists 6%. If your settlement statement shows a different rate, price from your statement.

Does TikTok charge the fee on shipping?

Yes — the official formula’s base includes the shipping fee paid by the customer. That is why the shipping you charge is an input above, separate from your own shipping cost.
