How Mercari Japan fees work

Rates verified: November 24, 2026 on Mercari's official Japanese help pages · Selling fee 10% of the sale price, charged when the transaction completes · No listing fee · Bank payout ¥200 flat per request (express payout adds ¥200).

The fee base is the listed price — all of it. Most Japanese sellers list with shipping included （送料込み）, and the 10% is charged on the full listed price, so the shipping portion is fee'd too. On a ¥10,000 listing with ¥750 shipping, the fee is ¥1,000 — not ¥925. If the buyer pays shipping separately, the fee falls on the item price only. Either way, fractions of a yen in the fee are truncated: a ¥999 sale pays a ¥99 fee.

The payout fee rewards batching. The ¥200 is per request, at any amount and any bank, so withdrawing after every sale quietly eats small sales alive — on a ¥1,000 sale it is another 20%. Leaving proceeds in your Merpay balance and spending them there costs nothing; the fee only appears when you cash out to a bank.

Not the same platform as Mercari US. Mercari Japan and Mercari US are separate marketplaces with separate accounts, and their fee bases differ: the US fee is 10% of the item price plus buyer-paid shipping, while Japan charges 10% of the listed price. Selling on Mercari Japan is a domestic arrangement — it generally requires a Japan-based account and a Japanese bank account for payouts. The US version has its own Mercari fee calculator.

Frequently asked questions

What is Mercari Japan's selling fee?

A flat 10% of the sale price, charged when the transaction completes. Listing is free. Verified on Mercari's official Japanese help pages on November 24, 2026.

Is the 10% charged on shipping too?

If your listed price includes shipping （送料込み）, yes — the fee base is the whole listed price. If the buyer pays shipping on top of the item price, the fee is charged on the item price.

How much does it cost to withdraw my money?

A flat ¥200 per payout request, regardless of the amount or the bank. An express payout （お急ぎ振込） adds a further ¥200. Keeping the balance in Merpay and spending it there is free.

How is the selling fee rounded?

Fractions of a yen are truncated. A ¥999 sale pays ¥99, a ¥1,999 sale pays ¥199 — the fee is always the whole-yen floor of 10% of the price.

Is Mercari Japan the same as Mercari US?

No. They are separate marketplaces with separate accounts and different fee bases — the US charges 10% of price plus buyer-paid shipping; Japan charges 10% of the listed price. This page covers Japan; the US has its own calculator.
