How eBay fees work

Rates verified: November 18, 2026 on eBay’s official Seller Center / Help fee pages · Final value fee 13.6% on the first $7,500 of the total amount of the sale, 2.35% on the portion above $7,500 (most categories, per item), + per-order fee. The fee base includes sales tax. Payment processing is included — no separate PayPal fee.

It is tiered, not flat. For most categories eBay charges 13.6% on the total amount of the sale up to $7,500 (calculated per item), and 2.35% on the portion over $7,500. A flat-13.6% calculator overstates fees by $56.25 on an $8,000 sale. Per-order fee: $0.30 on orders of $10.00 or less, $0.40 above that — applied automatically here. Categories: eBay’s official range across categories is 2.5%–15.3% (e.g. Books & Magazines, Movies & TV and most Music are 15.3% up to $7,500, then 2.35%); this calculator uses the “most categories” schedule for sellers without a Store.

Top Rated Plus discount. Per eBay’s official Seller standards policy, a listing from a Top Rated seller that offers same- or 1-business-day handling and 30-day-or-longer free returns earns a 10% discount on the final value fee — calculated on the total amount of the sale, and not applied to the per-order fee. It is 10% off the fee, not 10 points off the rate: 13.6% becomes an effective 12.24%.

Frequently asked questions

What percentage does eBay take in 2026?

For most categories, 13.6% of the total amount of the sale (item price + shipping charged + sales tax) on the first $7,500 per item, and 2.35% on the portion above $7,500 — plus a per-order fee of $0.30 (orders of $10 or less) or $0.40. Rates verified on eBay’s official fee pages on November 18, 2026.

Does eBay charge its fee on shipping and tax?

Yes. The final value fee is calculated on the “total amount of the sale”, which is the item price plus the shipping you charge the buyer plus any sales tax collected.

How much are eBay fees on a $100 item?

With $10 shipping charged and no tax, the total amount is $110: the final value fee is $14.96 plus the $0.40 per-order fee = $15.36 in total fees. Try it in the calculator above — the $100 + $10 ship preset is already loaded.

What is the eBay international fee?

If your eBay registered address is outside the US, eBay adds an international fee on cross-border sales, charged on the same total amount of the sale. The rate depends on your registered country — for example 1.35% for Japan. Select your location in Advanced settings to model it.

Is there a separate PayPal fee on eBay sales?

No. Payment processing is included in the final value fee under eBay managed payments.
