How Depop fees work

Rates verified: November 18, 2026 on Depop’s official Help Centre (“Seller fees and charges”) · Sellers based in the US, UK and Australia pay no Depop selling fee. The seller-side cost is the Depop Payments processing fee: US 3.3% + $0.45, UK 2.9% + £0.30, charged on the item price + shipping + any applicable taxes. This calculator covers the US and UK schedules (Australia: 2.6% + A$0.30, same structure). Optional Boosted Listings (12% in US/UK) are not modelled.

Depop removed its US selling fee on July 18, 2024 (the UK had done the same for new listings in March 2024); buyers now pay a Marketplace fee at checkout instead. Pages that still show a 10% Depop selling fee are describing the old structure — see the fee-change history.

Frequently asked questions

Does Depop take a percentage of sales?

Not from US, UK or Australian sellers anymore — the selling fee was removed (US: July 2024). Sellers pay only the payment processing fee: 3.3% + $0.45 (US) or 2.9% + £0.30 (UK).

What does a $35 sale cost on Depop US?

Processing of 3.3% on $35 = $1.16 (rounded), plus $0.45 = $1.61 total seller fees. The preset above is already set to exactly this example.

What is the buyer-side Marketplace fee?

Buyers pay a Marketplace fee at checkout on top of the price. It does not come out of the seller’s proceeds, so it is not in this calculator’s fee total.

Are Boosted Listings included?

No — Boosting is an optional promotion (12% in the US/UK) and is not modelled. If you boost, treat it as an extra cost line on top of the result here.
