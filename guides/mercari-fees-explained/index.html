Mercari Fees in 2026: What US Sellers Actually Pay Now

The short answer: since January 6, 2025, Mercari US charges sellers one flat fee — 10% of the item price plus the shipping the buyer pays — and no payment processing fee on top. The 3.6% Buyer Protection fee you may see at checkout is paid by the buyer, not by you. On a $50 item with $5 shipping, your total seller fee is $5.50. The Mercari fee calculator runs exactly this schedule.

Worked example — a $50 item with $5 shipping

Fee base: $50 + $5 = $55. Selling fee: $55 × 10% = $5.50. Processing fee: $0. Buyer Protection: $55 × 3.6% = $1.98 — charged to the buyer, shown in the calculator for information only. If your item cost $20 and shipping cost you $5, your net profit is $55 − $5.50 − $25 = $24.50.

Why so many pages get Mercari wrong

Mercari’s structure has flipped twice in two years. In 2024 it removed the seller fee entirely and moved charges to the buyer side. On January 6, 2025 it reversed course: a flat 10% seller fee came back, the separate processing fee was removed, and a buyer-paid 3.6% Buyer Protection fee was introduced. Any page written during the 2024 experiment — “Mercari has no seller fees” — is now wrong, and there are many of them. Always check the date and the source: Mercari’s own Help Center articles, “FAQs about our new fee structure” and “Changes to our fee structure”, describe the current schedule. The change is also logged in this site’s fee-change history.

Common misunderstandings

“Mercari is free for sellers.” It was, briefly, in 2024. Since January 6, 2025 the seller fee is 10%.

“There is a 10% fee plus a processing fee.” No — the January 2025 structure removed the separate processing fee. 10% is the whole seller-side percentage.

“The 3.6% comes out of my payout.” Buyer Protection is charged to the buyer on top of the price. It never reduces what the seller receives.

Frequently asked questions

What is Mercari’s selling fee in 2026?

A flat 10% of the item price plus buyer-paid shipping, with no payment processing fee. In force since January 6, 2025, and verified on Mercari’s official Help Center on November 18, 2026.

Does Mercari charge the 10% on shipping?

Yes — the base is the completed item price plus the shipping the buyer pays. $50 + $5 shipping pays $5.50, not $5.00.

Is Mercari cheaper than eBay?

On the same $50 + $5 example with $26 of costs: Mercari leaves $24.50, eBay leaves $21.12, Etsy leaves $23.32. Run your own numbers in the comparison — the ranking changes with price and shipping.

When did Mercari bring back seller fees?

January 6, 2025, replacing the 2024 buyer-fee experiment.
