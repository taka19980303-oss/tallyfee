eBay Fees Explained (2026): Every Fee, With Worked Examples

The short answer: for most categories, eBay takes 13.6% of the total amount of the sale — item price + shipping charged + sales tax — on the first $7,500 per item, 2.35% on anything above that, plus a per-order fee of $0.30 or $0.40. On a typical $110 sale that is $15.36, an effective rate of about 14%. Every figure below can be checked against eBay’s official fee pages, and the eBay fee calculator runs the same schedule.

Worked example 1 — a $100 sale

Item price $100, shipping charged $10, no tax. Total amount of the sale: $110. Final value fee: $110 × 13.6% = $14.96. Per-order fee: $0.40. Total eBay fees: $15.36. If your item cost $40 and shipping cost you $8, your net profit is $110 − $15.36 − $48 = $46.64. Load the “$100 + $10 ship” preset in the calculator and you will see exactly these figures, line by line.

Worked example 2 — why the tier matters at $8,000

An $8,000 item (no shipping charged): the first $7,500 is charged at 13.6% = $1,020.00, and the remaining $500 at 2.35% = $11.75. Final value fee: $1,031.75 (plus the $0.40 per-order fee). A calculator that applies a flat 13.6% to the whole amount would show $1,088.00 — overstating your fees by $56.25 and potentially scaring you off a profitable sale. This is the single most common error in eBay fee calculators, and the reason the tier is modelled here.

Common misunderstandings

“eBay charges 13.6% of the item price.” It charges on the total amount of the sale — price + shipping charged + sales tax. Free shipping does not avoid the fee; it just moves the amount into the price.

“The rate is 13.25%.” It was — until February 14, 2025, when it rose to 13.6% (see the fee-change history). Pages still showing 13.25% understate your fees by $0.35 on every $100.

“Top Rated Plus takes 10 points off the rate.” It takes 10% off the fee: 13.6% becomes an effective 12.24%, and the per-order fee is never discounted.

“There is also a PayPal fee.” Not under eBay managed payments — processing is included in the final value fee.

Frequently asked questions

What is eBay’s fee on $1,000?

With no shipping or tax: $1,000 × 13.6% = $136.00, plus the $0.40 per-order fee = $136.40 in total fees.

Do all categories pay 13.6%?

No — eBay’s official range across categories is 2.5%–15.3%. Books & Magazines, Movies & TV and most Music are 15.3% up to $7,500. This guide and calculator use the “most categories” schedule for sellers without a Store; check eBay’s fee page for your category.

When did eBay raise its fee to 13.6%?

February 14, 2025, up from 13.25%. The per-order fee for orders over $10 had risen earlier, from $0.30 to $0.40 on March 15, 2024.

How can I verify these rates myself?

Open eBay’s official Seller Center fee page (linked in the badge at the top) and compare the worked examples above — they are designed to be checkable, line by line.
