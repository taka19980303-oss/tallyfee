TikTok Shop Fees Explained (2026): One Referral Fee, by Category

The short answer: TikTok Shop charges one referral fee per order — 6% of the fee base for most categories, 5% for fine jewelry and pre-owned items, and 3% on the portion of a collectible or pre-owned sale over $10,000. The base is the customer payment plus any TikTok-funded platform discount, minus tax — and it includes the shipping the buyer paid. There is no separate payment-processing fee. On a $100 sale at the standard rate, the fee is $6.00. The TikTok Shop fee calculator runs the same schedule.

Worked example 1 — TikTok’s own example

TikTok’s official category page works this example: customer payment $102, a TikTok-funded platform discount of $5, and $2 of tax. Fee base: $102 + $5 − $2 = $105. Referral fee at the standard 6%: $6.30. Note the direction of the discount: because TikTok funded it, it is added back into the base — it does not reduce the fee, and it does not reduce what you receive. Enter $102 / $5 / $2 in the calculator and you will see the same $6.30.

Worked example 2 — a $12,000 pre-owned sale

A pre-owned watch sells with a fee base of $12,000 (no platform discount, no tax). The first $10,000 is charged at the pre-owned rate of 5% = $500.00, and the remaining $2,000 at the high-value tier of 3% = $60.00. Total referral fee: $560.00 — an effective rate of about 4.7%. A flat 5% would have been $600, and a flat 6% $720: on high-value collectible and pre-owned sales, the tier is worth real money, and calculators that skip it overstate the fee.

Common misunderstandings

“TikTok Shop charges 8% now.” Several seller blogs reported an 8% standard rate from August 2026. TikTok’s official category table, checked on November 25, 2026, still lists 6% for standard categories, and TikTok’s policy is to notify material fee changes in advance. Your own settlement statement (Seller Center → Finance → Statements) is the final word on what your shop was actually charged.

“The fee is charged on the item price.” It is charged on customer payment + platform discount − tax, and the base includes the shipping fee paid by the customer. Free shipping does not shrink the base; it moves the amount into the price.

“There is a payment-processing fee on top.” No — TikTok defines the referral fee as encompassing all TikTok Shop fees, with the exception of shipping and tax fees. There is no separate processing line on the settlement report.

“New sellers pay 3%.” Promotional rates (for example new-seller plans shown in Seller Center) are account-specific and time-limited. This guide and the calculator model the published category table, not promotions.

Frequently asked questions

How much does TikTok Shop take from a sale?

One referral fee: 6% of the fee base for most categories, 5% for fine jewelry and pre-owned items, and 3% on the portion of a collectible or pre-owned sale over $10,000. The fee base is what the customer paid (item + shipping) plus any TikTok-funded platform discount, minus tax. On a $100 item with free shipping and no discount or tax, the fee is $6.00.

What is the TikTok Shop referral fee charged on?

On customer payment + platform discount − tax, per TikTok’s official formula. The base includes the shipping fee paid by the customer, and a discount funded by TikTok is added back rather than subtracted.

Is there a payment processing fee on TikTok Shop?

No. TikTok’s policy defines the referral fee as encompassing all TikTok Shop fees, with the exception of shipping and tax fees — there is no separate processing line. Creator (affiliate) commissions are different: you set that rate yourself.

Did TikTok Shop raise its fee to 8%?

Not on the official category table checked on November 25, 2026, which still lists 6% for standard categories. Some seller posts report being charged 8% since August 2026; the rate on your own settlement statement in Seller Center is the one that applies to your shop.
