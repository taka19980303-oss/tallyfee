Poshmark Fees Explained (2026): $2.95 Under $15, 20% Above

The short answer: Poshmark’s commission is the simplest in this suite — a flat $2.95 on sales under $15, and 20% on sales of $15 or more, charged on the item price only. Shipping is a buyer-paid prepaid label, so it never enters the fee base, and there is no listing fee or separate payment-processing fee. On a $50 sale the commission is $10.00. The Poshmark fee calculator runs the same two steps.

Worked example 1 — a $50 sale

A dress sells for $50. At $15 or more, the commission is 20%: $50 × 20% = $10.00. You keep $40.00 before your own costs — no processing fee, per-order fee or shipping deduction to model on top. Run $50 in the calculator and the same $10.00 appears.

Worked example 2 — the $15 cliff

The two steps meet awkwardly at $15. A $14.99 sale pays the flat $2.95 — an effective 19.7%. A $15.00 sale pays 20% = $3.00. The true crossover is $14.75, the price at which $2.95 equals exactly 20%: below it, the flat fee takes a growing share as prices fall — on a $10 sale $2.95 is 29.5% of the price, and on a $5 sale it is 59%. Pricing a low-value item at $14.99 instead of $15.00 is the one place the cliff works in your favour; pricing at $5 means Poshmark keeps more than half.

Common misunderstandings

“Poshmark takes 20% of everything.” Under $15 it takes a flat $2.95 — which is more than 20% on anything priced under $14.75, and less than 20% only in the narrow band from $14.75 to $15.00.

“Commission is charged on shipping too.” No. Poshmark shipping is a prepaid label paid by the buyer; it never passes through your balance, so it is not revenue and not part of the commission base.

“Poshmark has a buyer protection fee.” It did, briefly: in October 2024 Poshmark moved to a different structure with fees on the buyer side, then reverted to the $2.95 / 20% commission within weeks (see the fee-change history). The two-step commission is the structure in force today.

“There is also a processing fee.” No — payment processing is inside the commission. The 20% (or $2.95) is the whole platform charge on a sale.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Poshmark take from a sale?

$2.95 flat on sales under $15, and 20% of the item price on sales of $15 or more. Rates verified on Poshmark’s official Support Center on November 25, 2026.

Does Poshmark charge commission on shipping?

No. Poshmark shipping is a prepaid label paid by the buyer; it never passes through your balance, so it is not revenue and not part of the commission base.

What is Poshmark’s fee on a $5 sale?

The flat $2.95 — which is 59% of a $5 price. The flat fee equals 20% at $14.75; below that price it takes a larger share the cheaper the item gets.

Didn’t Poshmark change its fees in 2024?

Yes — in October 2024 it briefly replaced the commission with a structure that included a buyer-side fee, then reverted to $2.95 / 20% within weeks. The reverted structure is the one in force today.
