Etsy Fees Explained (2026): The Four-Part Fee Stack

The short answer: a US Etsy sale pays four charges — a $0.20 listing fee, a 6.5% transaction fee on price + shipping, payment processing of 3% + $0.25 on the total the buyer paid (tax included), and nothing else. On a $100 order with free shipping that totals $9.95. Sellers in nine other countries also pay a regulatory operating fee; US sellers do not. The Etsy fee calculator applies each fee on its correct base.

✓ Rates verified November 18, 2026 — checked against the platform's official fee pages. Source: Etsy Fees & Payments Policy

The fee table

FeeRateCharged on
Listing fee$0.20Per listing, billed for 4 months or until the item sells
Transaction fee6.5%Item price + shipping + gift wrap — excluding sales tax (US)
Payment processing3% + $0.25The full amount the buyer paid — including sales tax
Regulatory operating fee0.05%–1.97%Price + shipping + gift wrap — nine countries only; US not charged
Offsite Ads (only if attributed)12% / 15%Price + shipping, capped at $100 per order

Worked example — a $100 order

Item price $100, free shipping, no tax. Listing fee $0.20. Transaction fee: $100 × 6.5% = $6.50. Processing: $100 × 3% + $0.25 = $3.25. Regulatory fee (US): $0. Total Etsy fees: $9.95 — an effective rate just under 10% before any advertising. Add $8 of sales tax and only the processing line moves: 3% of $108 + $0.25 = $3.49, because the transaction fee never touches tax for US sellers. Both cases are preset-checkable in the calculator.

The regulatory operating fee, country by country

Etsy charges this fee in exactly nine countries, at the schedule in force since its June 22, 2026 update: Hungary 1.97%, Türkiye 1.67%, Vietnam 1.24%, France 1.14%, Spain 0.88%, Italy 0.80%, Canada 0.50%, United Kingdom 0.48%, India 0.05%. The United States is not on the list — if a calculator adds a regulatory fee to a US sale, it is wrong. Etsy’s own Help Center article, “What is a Regulatory Operating Fee?”, is the source for the country list.

Common misunderstandings

“Etsy takes 6.5%.” That is one of four charges. With the listing fee and processing, a typical US order loses just under 10% to fees before advertising.

“Offsite Ads is 15% of everything.” It is 15% (or 12% for larger shops) of price + shipping only, only when a sale is attributed to an ad, and capped at $100 per order.

“The transaction fee rose recently.” The 6.5% rate dates from April 11, 2022 (up from 5%). What changed recently is the regulatory schedule — June 22, 2026 — which is why verification dates matter; see the fee-change history.

Frequently asked questions

How much are Etsy fees on a $50 item?

With $5 shipping and no tax: $0.20 + 6.5% of $55 ($3.58) + 3% of $55 + $0.25 ($1.90) = $5.68 total. That is the calculator’s default example.

Does Etsy charge fees on shipping?

Yes — both the transaction fee and the processing fee are charged on price + shipping. Only sales tax is excluded, and only from the transaction fee.

Can I opt out of Offsite Ads?

Shops with under $10,000 in prior-year sales can opt out. At $10,000 or more, participation is mandatory — but the fee is still capped at $100 per attributed order.

When did Etsy’s transaction fee become 6.5%?

April 11, 2022, up from 5% — the increase that prompted the April 2022 seller strike.
