Mercari Japan Fees Explained (2026): The 10% Selling Fee and the ¥200 Payout

The short answer: Mercari Japan — the original Japanese marketplace at mercari.jp, a separate platform from Mercari US — charges a flat 10% selling fee on the sale price when a transaction completes. Listing is free. Cashing out to a bank account costs a flat ¥200 per payout request, whatever the amount. On a ¥10,000 sale shipped for ¥750 and cashed out on its own, you keep ¥8,050. The Mercari Japan fee calculator runs the same numbers, in yen.

Worked example 1 — a ¥10,000 sale

You list at ¥10,000 with shipping included （送料込み） and ship by らくらくメルカリ便 宅急便 60-size for ¥750. Selling fee: ¥10,000 × 10% = ¥1,000 — charged on the whole listed price, shipping portion included. Credited to your balance: ¥9,000. After your ¥750 shipping cost: ¥8,250. Cash out to your bank in a single request and the ¥200 payout fee leaves a net of ¥8,050. The calculator shows exactly these figures with the same inputs.

Worked example 2 — small sales and the payout trap

Two details decide what small sales actually earn. First, rounding: fractions of a yen in the selling fee are truncated, so a ¥999 sale pays a ¥99 fee, not ¥99.90. Second, the payout fee is per request, not per sale: a ¥1,000 sale cashed out on its own pays ¥100 in selling fee plus ¥200 to withdraw — ¥300, or 30% of the price, before shipping. Withdraw after several sales instead, and the same ¥200 is spread across all of them. Leaving the balance in Merpay and spending it there avoids the fee entirely.

Common misunderstandings

“The 10% is charged on the item price only.” The base is the listed price — all of it. Most Japanese listings are 送料込み (shipping included), so the shipping portion is charged too: on a ¥10,000 listing with ¥750 of shipping inside it, the fee is ¥1,000, not ¥925. If the buyer pays shipping on top of the item price, the fee falls on the item price.

“Mercari Japan is the same platform as Mercari US.” They are separate marketplaces with separate accounts and different fee bases: the US charges 10% of the item price plus buyer-paid shipping, while Japan charges 10% of the listed price. The US version has its own guide and calculator.

“The ¥200 is charged per sale.” It is charged per payout request, at any amount. Batch your withdrawals — or keep the balance in Merpay, which is free.

“Listing costs money.” Listing is free. The selling fee is charged only when a transaction completes.

Frequently asked questions

What is Mercari Japan’s selling fee?

A flat 10% of the sale price, charged when the transaction completes. Listing is free. Verified on Mercari’s official Japanese help pages on November 25, 2026.

Is the 10% charged on shipping too?

If your listed price includes shipping （送料込み）, yes — the fee base is the whole listed price. If the buyer pays shipping on top of the item price, the fee is charged on the item price.

How much does it cost to withdraw my money?

A flat ¥200 per payout request, regardless of the amount or the bank. An express payout （お急ぎ振込） adds a further ¥200. Keeping the balance in Merpay and spending it there is free.

How is the selling fee rounded?

Fractions of a yen are truncated. A ¥999 sale pays ¥99, a ¥1,999 sale pays ¥199 — the fee is always the whole-yen floor of 10% of the price.
