KDP Printing Cost Calculator

KDP prints each copy on demand and takes the printing cost out of your royalty. The cost is a fixed cost plus a per-page cost — and for short books, a flat rate with no per-page charge at all. Pick your format, ink, marketplace and page count.

How KDP printing costs work

Rates verified: November 23, 2026 on KDP’s official printing-cost pages · Printing cost = fixed cost + (page count × per-page cost) · Short books pay the fixed cost only: black-ink paperbacks up to 110 pages, groundwood up to 112, premium color up to 40, black-ink hardcovers up to 108.

The flat band is free pages. A black-ink paperback sold on Amazon.com costs $2.30 to print whether it has 24 pages or 110 — every page up to 110 costs nothing extra. Above the band, the fixed cost drops and a per-page charge takes over: a 300-page book costs $1.00 + 300 × $0.012 = $4.60. That example is KDP’s own, and the defaults above are set to it so you can check the arithmetic.

Ink is the biggest lever. On Amazon.com at regular trim, each page costs $0.012 in black ink, $0.0255 in standard color and $0.065 in premium color — premium color pages cost more than five times black. Groundwood paper undercuts white/cream by about 5% per page. Trim size matters too: anything wider than 6.12 in or taller than 9 in pays the large-trim rate on every copy.

Scope, stated plainly. Paperback costs are modelled for all eight marketplaces in KDP’s table (US, Canada, UK, EU stores, Poland, Sweden, Australia, Japan). Standard color is not offered on Amazon.com.au, so it is not modelled there. Hardcover costs are modelled for Amazon.com, Amazon.co.uk and the EU stores — the marketplaces whose full hardcover tables we verified; KDP does not sell hardcovers on Amazon.co.jp at all.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to print a 300-page paperback on KDP?

On Amazon.com, in black ink at a regular trim size: $1.00 fixed + 300 × $0.012 per page = $4.60 per copy. The same book as a hardcover costs $9.25 — the per-page rate is identical, the fixed cost is $4.65 higher.

Why does my 100-page book cost the same as a 24-page book?

Black-ink paperbacks from 24 to 110 pages pay only the fixed cost ($2.30 on Amazon.com, regular trim) — there is no per-page charge inside the band. Shortening a 100-page book to 90 pages saves nothing per copy.

What is the minimum list price KDP allows?

Printing cost ÷ royalty rate. Because the print royalty rate is 60% at or above the marketplace threshold ($9.99 on Amazon.com) and 50% below it, the minimum is whichever of the two calculations lands in its own price range — this calculator works out which one applies.

Do bleed or cover finish change the printing cost?

No. KDP states that bleed settings and cover finish (glossy or matte) do not affect printing cost. Page count, ink, paper, trim class and marketplace are the only inputs.

Does KDP charge printing cost upfront?

No. There is no upfront charge and no inventory: each copy is printed when ordered, and the printing cost is subtracted from that sale’s royalty.
