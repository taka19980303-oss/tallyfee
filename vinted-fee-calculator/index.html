How Vinted fees work

Rates verified: November 18, 2026 on Vinted’s official Price List and Help Centre · Seller fee: $0. The buyer pays a Vinted fee (usually 5% + $0.70 in the US) and pays shipping directly, so neither passes through the seller.

“No seller fee” is Vinted’s whole model: the platform monetises the buyer side. For sellers the comparison that matters is not fees but net after your own costs — and how the same item does on platforms that do charge, which is what the 8-platform comparison shows with your own numbers.

Frequently asked questions

Does Vinted take a cut of my sale?

No. Vinted charges sellers no selling fee, commission or processing fee. The buyer pays the Vinted fee (usually 5% + $0.70 in the US) and the shipping.

Why is there a calculator if the fee is $0?

Because your profit is not the sale price: item cost and any shipping you absorb still come out of it. The calculator keeps those honest — and shows the buyer-side fee for reference, clearly marked as not deducted from you.

Who pays for shipping on Vinted?

The buyer, directly, at checkout. That is why the default shipping cost here is $0; change it only if you personally absorb a shipping cost.
