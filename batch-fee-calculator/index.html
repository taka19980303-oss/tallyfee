How the batch defaults work

The batch uses each platform’s standard case, the same defaults as the eight-platform comparison: eBay with no sales tax, no Promoted Listings and a US seller; Etsy with a US seller and no Offsite Ads sale; Amazon’s referral fee only, in the Everything Else category (15%) — open the Amazon calculator when you need another category. If a row needs the full detail — Top Rated Plus, an international sale, a different Etsy regulatory country — price that item on its own platform page, where every option is exposed.

Totals are plain sums of the rounded per-row figures shown in the table, so the totals row always equals the sum of the rows above it — and of the CSV you download. The CSV adds a revenue column per row and ends with a TOTAL line for fees and profit, ready to paste into the spreadsheet where you track your stock.
