How KDP royalties work

Rates verified: November 23, 2026 on KDP’s official Help · Print: (rate × list price) − printing cost, rate 60% at/above the marketplace threshold, 50% below, 40% on Expanded Distribution · eBook 70% band on Amazon.com: $2.99–$12.99 since July 7, 2026.

The $9.99 cliff is real. KDP changed print royalties in June 2025: the rate is no longer a flat 60%. Price a 300-page black-ink paperback at $9.99 and you earn 0.60 × $9.99 − $4.60 = $1.39. Price it one cent lower, at $9.98, and the 50% rate applies: 0.50 × $9.98 − $4.60 = $0.39. One cent of list price moves the royalty by a dollar. This calculator flags the cliff whenever your price sits below the threshold.

Thresholds by marketplace (list price at or above which 60% applies): Amazon.com $9.99 · Amazon.co.uk £7.99 · EU stores €9.99 · Amazon.ca C$13.99 · Amazon.com.au A$13.99 · Amazon.co.jp ¥1,000 · Amazon.pl 40 PLN · Amazon.se 110 SEK. Hardcovers use the same rates and formula, with their own higher printing costs — and no Expanded Distribution.

eBooks: the band widened in 2026. The 70% option requires a list price inside the store’s band — on Amazon.com, $2.99–$12.99 since July 7, 2026 (the cap was $9.99 from 2007 until then). Inside the band you earn 70% of the price after VAT, minus a delivery cost charged per megabyte of file size ($0.15/MB on Amazon.com, £0.10 in the UK, €0.12 in the EU stores, ¥1 in Japan — and no delivery cost at all in Japan for files of 10 MB or more). Outside the band, or for public-domain titles, the 35% option applies with no delivery cost. The 70% price must also sit at least 20% below any print edition’s price.

Frequently asked questions

How much does KDP pay per book sold?

For print: (royalty rate × list price) − printing cost. A 300-page black-ink paperback at $14.99 on Amazon.com earns 0.60 × $14.99 − $4.60 = $4.39 per sale. For eBooks: 70% of the price after VAT and delivery costs inside the price band, otherwise 35% of the price after VAT.

Why did my paperback royalty drop below $9.99?

Since June 2025 the print royalty rate is tiered: 60% at or above $9.99 on Amazon.com, 50% below it. A book priced at $9.98 earns the 50% rate on the whole price — not a blend — which is why one cent can cost about a dollar per sale on a mid-length book.

What is the KDP minimum list price?

Printing cost ÷ royalty rate, worked out so the royalty always covers printing. Because the rate changes at the threshold, the minimum can sit in either tier — this calculator determines which tier applies and shows the exact figure.

How are eBook delivery costs calculated?

File size in megabytes × the store’s per-MB rate, under the 70% option only: $0.15/MB on Amazon.com, £0.10 on Amazon.co.uk, €0.12 in the euro stores, ¥1 on Amazon.co.jp (waived there for files of 10 MB or more), with a minimum of $0.01 (¥1 in Japan). The 35% option has no delivery cost.

Do hardcovers earn the same royalty as paperbacks?

The formula and the 60%/50% tiers are the same, but hardcover printing costs are much higher — a 300-page black-ink hardcover costs $9.25 to print against the paperback’s $4.60 — so the same list price pays noticeably less. Hardcovers also have no Expanded Distribution channel.
