How Amazon referral fees work

Rates verified: November 18, 2026 on Amazon’s official pricing page (sell.amazon.com) · This calculator covers the referral fee only, by category. The referral fee is a percentage of the total sales price (item price + shipping + gift-wrap charges), or the category minimum, whichever is greater. Out of scope, by design: selling-plan fees (Individual $0.99/item or Professional $39.99/month), FBA fulfilment and storage fees, and closing fees other than Media’s — those vary by product and fulfilment choice.

The category decides the rate. Per Amazon’s official pricing page: “For every item sold, sellers pay Amazon a percentage of the total price, or a minimum amount, whichever is greater.” Most everyday categories — Home & Kitchen, Toys, Sports, Tools, Everything Else — are 15% with a $0.30 minimum. Consumer Electronics and Computers are 8%. Clothing is bracketed by total price: 5% at $15 or less, 10% above $15 up to $20, 17% above $20 (the bracket rate applies to the whole total). Jewelry is portion-tiered: 20% on the portion up to $250, 5% above. Media pays 15% plus a $1.80 closing fee per item.

Frequently asked questions

What is Amazon’s referral fee?

A category-based percentage of the total sales price (item price + shipping + gift wrap), or a minimum amount ($0.30 in most categories), whichever is greater. Most everyday categories are 15%; Consumer Electronics and Computers are 8%.

Does Amazon charge the referral fee on shipping?

Yes — the base is the total sales price, which includes the shipping you charge and any gift-wrap charges.

What is not included in this calculator?

Selling-plan fees ($0.99/item Individual or $39.99/month Professional), FBA fulfilment and storage fees, and closing fees other than Media’s $1.80. Those depend on your plan and fulfilment choices, so they are deliberately out of scope and stated as such.

How is Clothing’s referral fee calculated?

By bracket: the rate is set by the total sales price — 5% at $15 or less, 10% above $15 up to $20, 17% above $20 — and that single rate applies to the whole total, unlike Jewelry or Watches where each rate applies only to its portion.
