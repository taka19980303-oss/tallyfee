How Whatnot fees work

Rates verified: November 18, 2026 on Whatnot’s official Help Center (“Whatnot seller fees”) · US Standard tier: commission 8% of the item’s final sale price (4% for Coins & Money) — charged on the sale price only, excluding shipping and taxes. Payment processing is separate: 2.9% + $0.30 on the total order value (item + buyer-paid shipping + taxes). Since September 21, 2026, commission drops in tiers as your four-week sales grow; this calculator uses the Standard tier every new seller starts on, and says so.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Whatnot charge sellers?

On the Standard tier: 8% commission on the item price (4% for Coins & Money), plus payment processing of 2.9% + $0.30 on the total order value including shipping and tax.

Is commission charged on shipping?

No — commission is on the item’s final sale price only. Processing is the charge that applies to the order total (item + shipping + tax).

What are Whatnot’s commission tiers?

Since September 21, 2026, commission falls in tiers as your trailing four-week sales grow; the Standard tier ($0–$14,999) is 8% in most verticals. This calculator uses the Standard tier and labels it — check Seller Hub for your live tier.
